Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

The short version: Cold or Allergy has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your log stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We never receive it.

What the app stores

The check-ins you create (how you feel, symptoms, medication, time outdoors, notes), the environmental readings recorded alongside them, and your weekly summaries. This data is stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it's available on your iPhone and iPad. It's protected by your Apple Account, and the developer has no access to it.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location while you're using it (and for its widgets) to fetch pollen, air quality and weather. Before any request, your coordinates are rounded to roughly one kilometer. The approximate location is sent directly from your device to these providers:

  • Google Maps Platform Pollen API for pollen levels (Google Privacy Policy)
  • U.S. EPA AirNow for air quality (EPA Privacy Notice)
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for weather (Apple Privacy Policy)

No other information about you is sent with these requests, and the developer doesn't receive or store your location. The city name shown in the app and included in exports comes from Apple's on-device geocoding.

Apple Intelligence

Insights, including the weekly summary and the chat, are generated by Apple's on-device foundation model. Your data isn't sent to the developer, to Apple's servers, or to any third-party AI service for this feature. Weekly summaries are saved with the rest of your log (on your device and in your private iCloud). Chats aren't saved; they disappear when you close them.

Exports

When you export your data, you choose where it goes (for example Files, Mail or a chat assistant). Once shared, that copy is handled by the app or service you send it to.

Purchases

The app is purchased through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the developer doesn't receive your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders and the weekly summary notification are scheduled locally on your device. Logging a face from a notification saves it on your device. No push notification service is used to track you.

Children

Cold or Allergy isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Cold or Allergy is a personal logging tool. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Consult a healthcare professional about your symptoms.

Deleting your data

Delete individual entries in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app and then deleting its data in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
