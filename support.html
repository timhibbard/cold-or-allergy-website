Frequently asked questions

How do I log how I feel?

Tap one of the five faces on the Today screen, a widget, or a reminder notification. That's it: the check-in is saved. You can then add symptoms, medication, time outdoors and a note, but they're optional.

Why does the app need my location?

To look up pollen, air quality and weather where you are. Your location is rounded to about a kilometer before it's sent to the data providers, and it's never sent to us. See the Privacy Policy.

Pollen or air quality isn't showing.

Make sure Location is allowed for Cold or Allergy in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, and that you're online. Air quality is available in the United States only.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync?

On your iPhone and in your private iCloud account, so it's backed up and syncs between your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. We can't see it.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings → Export in the app. You can export JSON, CSV, or an AI-ready summary to paste into a chat assistant.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries in the app, or delete everything by removing the app and then going to iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Cold or Allergy → Delete Data.

Can the app tell me whether I have a cold or allergies?

No. Cold or Allergy helps you notice patterns, but it isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. Talk to a doctor about your symptoms.
